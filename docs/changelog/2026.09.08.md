I recently finished mobile navigation. It was a lot trickier than I had initially anticipated, I had to develop an entirely new viewing mode, the biggest challenge being [[2026-09_layout-refactor | figuring out how to keep the table of contents and spreads in sync]].

I've started working on the subtitles, as inspired by https://josephpleass.com. The initial implementation was pretty straightforward, just a [`setInterval()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/setInterval) with a timer connected to an array of lines to be shown. Since the transitions are triggered by `pageStore.set()`, the mobile version suffers from a delay caused by the page-change event listener only being triggered on scroll end, [tracked here](https://github.com/kikidotdirectory/light-green-notebook/issues/7).
